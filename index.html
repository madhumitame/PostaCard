Builder · Writer · Postcard Lover

💌

So many of my friends collect postcards as souvenirs when they travel — and it got me thinking.💌

Letting my friend know I thought of her when I saw a sunset at St Ives beach through a text message just isn't cutting it. And sending an actual postcard from abroad? Time constraints, language barriers, it's a whole thing.

So I built PostaCard — come back home and send your own personalised postcard to anyone around the world. Hope you like it! 🌍
